# Third-party assets and source licensing

No project-wide license has been selected for this private development source.
Public distribution remains blocked until licensing and asset rights are
confirmed; the font licenses below do not license the extension source.

## Fonts

Both font binaries were copied from Agent Zero's bundled `webui/vendor/fonts`
assets. Their adjacent upstream SIL Open Font License 1.1 files are included in
`public/fonts` and are carried into built packages. The exact binary hashes,
rather than an unverified upstream version or commit, identify these copies.
